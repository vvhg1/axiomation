## Where you write

`edges.md` holds the edges and standings a reader wrote before you: read
it, and do not edit it. Write only your changes, in `lines.md`, by
owner. This section takes the place of the opening's "Edit `edges.md`
alone; it is your answer" and of § "The task".

- For each block or group whose lines you change, a heading
  `@@ <handle>`, then one line a change:
  - `+ <line>` adds a line, written as § "The sheet" writes it;
  - `- <line>` removes a line the sheet holds under that owner: copy it
    whole, or an edge's or a standing's head and quote alone
    (`- → uses <target> · "<evidence>"`, `- ⊢ <status> · "<claim>"`);
  - to correct a line, remove it and add it corrected.
- The parts' legend lines and the `Pervasive:` line change the same way,
  under one heading `@@ the header`, first: `+ <target> [<label>]:
  "<words>"`, and `- Pervasive: …` then `+ Pervasive: …`.
- An owner you do not name keeps its lines as they are. Write no
  block's done mark (`⟦done <handle>⟧`) and no block text.
- A claim standing `unsupported` that a line you add argues (a justifies
  line onto it, a self mark) takes the standing that line gives it; you
  need not change its ⊢ line.
- When you try to finish, a check applies `lines.md` to the sheet and
  reads it whole. It hands back a change that does not apply (a removed
  line its owner does not hold, an added one it holds already) and the
  lines that do not read. Fix those, keep the rest, then finish.
- When every change is written, end `lines.md` with `⟦done⟧` on its own
  line. A pass that finds nothing to change writes that line alone.
