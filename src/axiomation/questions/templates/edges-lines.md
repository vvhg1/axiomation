## Where you write

`edges.md` is the sheet to read: do not edit it. Write your answer in
`lines.md`, by owner. This section takes the place of the opening's "Edit
`edges.md` alone; it is your answer" and of § "The task".

- For every block and every group, a line `@@ <handle>`, then that
  owner's lines as § "The sheet" writes them (its edges, its self marks,
  its claims' standings, its cut lines and flags), each on its own line.
  A block with no line has its heading alone. The heading is the owner's
  done mark: write no `⟦done …⟧` line, and no block text.
- Parts' legend lines and the `Pervasive:` line go under one heading
  `@@ the header`, first.
- When you try to finish, a check lays `lines.md` into the sheet and reads
  it whole. It hands back the lines that do not read, the blocks and groups
  with no heading, and a heading that names neither. Fix those, keep the
  rest, then finish. Write every owner before you finish.
